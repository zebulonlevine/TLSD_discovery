## Methods text

**Live-cell imaging.** [Cell line] L29 cells expressing GFP-R3HDM4 were labeled with JF552 (Sec61B, ER) and JFX650 (L29, 60S ribosomal subunit) [tag/ligand, concentrations] on 2 April 2026. They were treated with PT179 (20 µM) or DMSO, then imaged live [approx. 11 h after treatment; files are labeled "23h"]. Images were acquired on a Nikon Ti2 inverted microscope with a CrestOptics X-Light spinning-disk confocal (70 µm pinhole), using an Apo LWD 40×/1.15 NA water-immersion objective (λS), Lumencor Celesta laser excitation, a penta-band excitation filter (Semrock FF01-391/477/549/639/741) and dichroic (Semrock FF421/491/567/659/776-Di01), and a Hamamatsu ORCA-Fusion sCMOS camera. The three channels were acquired sequentially (laser power as % of maximum output):
- GFP: 488 nm excitation, 100% power, 500 ms exposure, FITC emission filter [bandpass]
- JF552: 545 nm excitation, 50% power, 1 s exposure, Cy3 emission filter [bandpass]
- JFX650: 640 nm excitation, 50% power, 400 ms exposure, Cy5 emission filter [bandpass]

Acquisition settings were identical for the DMSO and PT179 images.

Z-stacks were 43 planes at 0.4 µm spacing, with a pixel size of 0.1625 µm and a 2304 × 2304 px field. Focus was held with Perfect Focus. On-camera Denoise.ai was off. Raw stacks were then denoised offline with NIS-Elements Batch Denoise [confirm module/version].

**Cell segmentation.** Because no nuclear or membrane marker was present, a single segmentation image was generated by normalizing each channel to its 99.9th percentile and taking the per-pixel maximum across channels. Cells were segmented with Cellpose-SAM (`cpsam` pretrained model, Cellpose v4.0.9) applied to each z-plane in 2D (diameter = 260 px, flow threshold = 0.4, cell-probability threshold = 0), and per-plane masks were stitched into 3D objects using Cellpose's IoU-based stitching (threshold = 0.5). Objects smaller than 500 voxels were discarded.

| 4 | 2D, per plane | Cellpose-SAM network predicts flows + cell probability; outputs resized back to full size | — | Cellpose |
| 5 | 2D, per plane | Flows → masks; pixels below the probability threshold dropped; masks with poor flow agreement dropped; masks > 40% of the plane dropped; minimum size **not** applied yet (turned off when stitching) | cellprob = 0, flow = 0.4 | Cellpose |

**Colocalization.** For each image, each channel was clipped to the 0.1st–99.9th percentile of its nonzero voxels to limit the influence of hot pixels and saturated voxels. For every segmented cell of ≥500 voxels, Pearson's correlation coefficient (r) was computed between each pair of channels across all voxels inside that cell's 3D mask (`scipy.stats.pearsonr`). No background subtraction or subcellular masking was applied.

**Statistics.** Each cell is one data point. The DMSO and PT179 distributions were compared with a two-sided Mann-Whitney U test (`scipy.stats.mannwhitneyu`). Horizontal bars show medians. Each condition is one field of view from one dish [confirm], so p-values describe cell-to-cell variation within one experiment, not replicate experiments.

**Software.** Python 3.10 with Cellpose 4.0.9 (PyTorch 2.10.0), nd2 0.11.2, NumPy 2.2.6, SciPy 1.15.2, scikit-image 0.25.2, pandas 2.3.3, tifffile 2025.5.10, matplotlib 3.10.8 and seaborn 0.13.2. The analysis ran on the Whitehead Slurm cluster (conda env `nd2_coloc`; `envs/nd2_coloc.yml`).

## Reproduction

See `README.md` for the environment variables.

```bash
bash submit_pearson_repro.sh                    # steps 2-3, reuse existing masks
bash submit_pearson_repro.sh --start-step 1 \
     --seg-out /path/to/new_output              # full rerun incl. Cellpose-SAM
```

| Step | Script | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `segment_cells.py` | Cellpose-SAM; also writes per-channel TIFs to `channels/` |
| 2 | `wholecell_pearson.py` | Gives the same values as the `whole_cell` rows of the original, superseded `quantify_coloc.py` (not shipped) |
| 3 | `plot_pearson_swarm.py` | Swarm plot, medians, Mann-Whitney U; writes `*_stats.csv` |

The ER masking (`make_er_mask.py`) and Manders/ICQ steps of the original 4-step pipeline are not needed: the whole-cell compartment in `quantify_coloc.py` does not use the ER mask.
